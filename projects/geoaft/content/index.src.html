GeoAFT

Geometric Attention Fine-Tuning

Adapting how representations relate to one another, not only the representations themselves.

A pretrained Transformer learns representations and a rule for comparing them. Fine-tuning usually changes the first. GeoAFT adapts the second.

GeoAFT at a glance

The idea

LoRA-style methods change how queries and keys are produced. Geometric attention fine-tuning changes how they are compared:

\[ S_{ij} = s_{\mathrm{base}}(q_i, k_j) + \alpha_h\, \bar g_\phi\big(P_q q_i,\; P_k k_j\big) \]

\(g_\phi\) is a small trainable geometry. The gate \(\alpha_h\) starts at zero, so training begins from exactly the pretrained attention.

Where this is going proposed

  1. When is geometry adaptation actually different?

    Pair every geometry family with a containment control, and go beyond today’s families to spherical, hyperbolic, mixed-curvature, local-metric, learned-kernel and dynamic geometries.

  2. Can geometry become conditional?

    Let the geometry depend on the input, layer, head or local statistics instead of one global metric.

  3. Does it survive beyond attention and under compression?

    Test JEPA-style latent prediction, encoder, vision and decoder-only models, and BF16 → INT8 → INT4 precision.
