About

Mi studies Accountancy and Data Science & Artificial Intelligence at Nanyang Technological University, with an interest in quantitative finance. Through URECA, Mi researches volatility pricing and leverage-decay risk in leveraged ETF options, and has worked on systematic investment strategies with the Nanyang Student Investment Fund. Technical interests include statistical modelling and using mathematical and computational methods to study financial markets.
