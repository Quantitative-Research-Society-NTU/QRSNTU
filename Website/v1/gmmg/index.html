FAQ

Who can participate?

The Singapore competition is open to university students across Singapore. You do not need to be an NTU student or an existing QRS member.

Is this a team competition?

No. Participants compete individually. You do not need to form a team.

Do I need prior trading experience?

The official Games materials do not list a prior-experience requirement. The Games focus on pricing, probability, estimation and mental arithmetic, and the official website shows each game in detail.

How much does it cost?

Entry is free.

What will I play?

The competition uses the Market Making Games platform from Trading Interview, with exercises in market making, probability, estimation, mental arithmetic and related quantitative decision-making. See the official Games page for full details.

When is the Singapore qualifier?

Friday 16 October 2026, 19:30–21:00 SGT.

What happens if I qualify?

Leading qualifiers progress to the Singapore Final on Friday 13 November. The Singapore champion then moves on to World Final day on 12 December, where a final qualifier fills the ten seats at the final table.

Where will the event take place?

Singapore. Venue details will be announced to registered participants once confirmed.

What should I bring?

Setup requirements have not been published yet. Registered participants will receive joining details, including anything to bring, once confirmed.

Who organises the competition?

The Global Market Making Games are hosted by Amsterdam Investment Club and powered by Trading Interview, with Flow Traders as Super Day partner. QRS@NTU is coordinating the Singapore participation as local partner.

How do I register?

Use the Register button at the top of this page. Registration is individual.
