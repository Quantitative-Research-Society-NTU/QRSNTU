About QRS

Our Vision

QRS@NTU(TW) develops and conducts student-led research in quantitative finance, machine learning, and related technical areas.

QRS and QRS@NTU(TW)

QRS is the Society's cross-institutional network. Research is conducted under the broader QRS identity to enable collaboration across universities and institutions.

QRS@NTU is its originating campus community at Nanyang Technological University, where student-led research is developed and conducted alongside NTU-based events and local activities.
