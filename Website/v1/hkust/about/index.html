About QRS@HKUST

Our Vision

A student research society at HKUST dedicated to quantitative research, public technical learning, and industry engagement in mathematics, finance, and computational sciences.

Core Focus Areas

Quantitative Research

Original research and projects spanning mathematics, machine learning, and quantitative finance. See Projects and Publications.

Public Technical Learning

Structured programmes for members at different stages, from Quant Foundations to the Research Academy.

Industry & External Activity

Industry talks, external workshops, and competitions, listed on the Events page.

Open Academic Resources

Share technical notes, research methods, and reproducible learning resources.

How QRS Operates

QRS's day-to-day work is organised around a small set of areas, with collaboration across HKUST and other campus societies:

  • Education
  • Research
  • Events & External Relations
  • Operations & Infrastructure
  • Cross-campus / society collaboration
