Programmes

Two structured paths into QRS: an introductory track and a research track.

Quant Foundations

A public-facing introductory quant programme, and the broadest entry point into QRS.

Planned direction

  • 4–6 weekly sessions
  • Introductory quantitative finance/research
  • Probability, Statistics & Data
  • Machine Learning / Time Series
  • Portfolio, Optimisation & Risk
  • How quant research is done in practice
  • Transition into QRS research/programme participation

Research Academy

A more serious recurring research programme focused on producing research, not introductory content.

Planned direction

  • Choosing/narrowing a research question
  • Literature review and novelty assessment
  • Theory vs. empirical contribution
  • Experimental design
  • Baselines, robustness and statistical validation
  • Reproducibility
  • Research writing
  • Venue selection
  • Internal review and revision
  • Project execution toward conference/workshop/journal submission
