Entry-stage research training at QRS@NTU, with an introductory programme under development.

QRS Research Academy

The entry-stage educational research programme. Participants progress from guided research towards independently formulating, executing, analysing, and writing their own research projects. The initial target is to lead a first first-author workshop paper under mentorship.

Beyond the Academy

Strong participants may progress into QRS Researcher roles, contributing to larger research projects and eventually leading full archival conference or journal papers.
