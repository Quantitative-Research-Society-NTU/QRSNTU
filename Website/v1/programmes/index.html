QRS Research Academy

A cohort-based research programme. You take on a focused research question, often from an existing QRS project, and work towards an external paper submission.

What you'll do

  • Define a research question
  • Review the literature and check novelty
  • Develop theory or experiments
  • Test against baselines
  • Get internal review
  • Write and submit to a workshop or conference
