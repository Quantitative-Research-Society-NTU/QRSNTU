## The puzzle (0:00–0:15)

GeoAFT: geometric attention fine-tuning. A pretrained Transformer learns two things: representations, and a rule for comparing them. Fine-tuning usually changes the representations. The rule that decides which tokens attend to which is simply inherited.

## The intuition (0:15–0:44)

Take one query and seven keys, and hold them still. Measure with Euclidean distance, and the nearest key wins. Bend the ruler into an L-p distance, and a different key takes the lead. Stretch and turn a learned metric, and the ranking shifts again. Cosine looks only at direction. The dot product, the pretrained default, rewards whatever lies furthest along the query. Nothing moved. Only the geometry changed.

## The method (0:44–0:58)

GeoAFT makes that geometry trainable. It keeps the pretrained score and adds a small, structured term on top. A gate starts at zero, so training begins from exactly the pretrained model.

## Tested so far (0:58–1:35)

So far we have tested it where the right answer is known: three synthetic targets. On each, GeoAFT recovers more than ninety-nine point nine percent of the gap to the oracle. Here is every method we ran, by trainable parameters and recovery. Low-rank Q/K adaptation stays about forty points behind. Generic learned scorers trail by six to twelve points, or need thousands of parameters to come close. One caution: on the L-p target a fixed geometry bank comes almost as close, so not every comparison is a clear win.

## What the results mean (1:35–1:53)

So a geometry update can be one of three things. An exact rewrite of something weights already do, a cheaper way to say the same thing, or something new. The first is proven, the second is supported, and the third is still open.

## In progress and next (1:53–2:12)

Real benchmarks are in progress. Next, we ask when geometry adaptation is actually different, across richer families such as spherical and hyperbolic geometry. Whether geometry can become conditional on the input, layer or head. And whether the idea survives beyond attention, and under compression.
