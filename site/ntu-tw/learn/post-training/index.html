Post-training

How a model that predicts the next word becomes an assistant that answers, follows instructions and reasons.

Pre-training teaches a language model what text looks like. Post-training reshapes what it chooses to say: first by imitating good answers, then by learning which of its own answers people prefer, and most recently by rewarding answers that can be checked.

  3. Compare

    Reward model

    Fit a scalar score to human comparisons with the Bradley–Terry model: the preferred reply should score higher.

    Reward model →

  4. Optimise

    RL with PPO

    Sample replies, score them, and push the policy towards high reward while a KL penalty keeps it near the SFT model.

    PPO →

  5. Departures

    DPO

    Skip the reward model and the RL loop: a classification-style loss on preference pairs, derived from the same objective.

    RLVR and GRPO

    Replace learned rewards with checkable ones (a correct maths answer, passing tests) and the critic with a group of samples.
